# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tourists and short-stay visitors planning a trip to Tbilisi, Georgia. Trilingual audience: English, Georgian, Russian. Context: pre-trip research and in-city discovery, mostly mobile or laptop, casual browsing mood.

## Product Purpose

AI Trip Planner — official-style city guide for Tbilisi: discover places, book experiences, find food, track events, buy transit tickets, and generate AI itineraries. Success = visitors find and book things to do without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, authentic, local. Like a knowledgeable local friend showing you the city — not a corporate booking engine.

## Anti-references

- Generic booking-site clutter (dense listings, aggressive CTAs, badge spam).
- Do NOT restyle the existing site look. The visual identity (DM Serif display, amber #E8A020 accent, existing `--site-*` tokens) is committed; changes must fit inside it.

## Design Principles

1. Identity preservation — work within the existing token system, never reinvent it.
2. Trilingual parity — every UI decision must work in en, ka, and ru; Georgian text runs wide, design for it.
3. City-portal clarity — navigation and naming follow visit-city conventions (VisitOslo, NYCgo), scannable over clever.
4. Warm over sterile — human copy and photography-led warmth, not SaaS minimalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, keyboard navigable, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
